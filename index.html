Key Legal Disclosures (already in each store's Policies page):

  • 14-day statutory right of withdrawal (cooling-off / distance selling) applies to EU, Sweden (distansavtalslagen), Norway (Angrerettloven), and UK (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013). Full refund of price + standard delivery cost. Customer pays return postage (except faulty/wrong item).
  • EU Withdrawal Button: As of 19 June 2026, EU online traders must provide an easy online withdrawal function. Policies include clear instructions and mock button flow.
  • Faulty goods: Separate statutory rights (up to 3 years in SE/EU, 2+ years UK) for repair/replacement/refund if not as described or unfit. Not limited by return window.
  • Hygiene/personal items: Sealed goods for health/hygiene cannot be returned once opened/seal broken (clearly disclosed).
  • Dropshipping disclosure: Products fulfilled direct from third-party suppliers. Delivery estimates 7–21 business days. No inventory held by us.
  • VAT & Pricing: Prices include applicable VAT (25% SE/NO, 20% UK). Import VAT/duties may apply on non-EU origin goods (disclosed at checkout where possible).
  • Company details: Placeholder legal entity info shown. Real business must display accurate registered name, address, org/VAT numbers, and email on every site.
  • No prohibited claims: No medical, therapeutic, or licensed-brand claims. All products are generic consumer goods.

Next steps for real use: Register proper company in relevant jurisdiction(s), set up real payment processor (Stripe etc with proper VAT handling), obtain IOSS if shipping low-value imports to EU, keep accurate records, and have a lawyer review your exact setup.
